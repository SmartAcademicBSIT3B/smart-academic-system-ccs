About the System

Thesis/Capstone Archiving and Host Training Establishment System

This system is designed to manage and archive thesis and capstone projects for the College of Computer Studies (CCS). It also provides a comprehensive platform for coordinators and administrators to oversee HTE (Host Training Establishment) partnerships, manage OJT (On-the-Job Training) students, and maintain archives of academic projects.

Key Features:
  • Dashboard for overview and analytics
  • Archive management for thesis and capstone projects
  • HTE partner management
  • OJT student tracking and management
  • User management and role assignments
  • System settings and configuration
